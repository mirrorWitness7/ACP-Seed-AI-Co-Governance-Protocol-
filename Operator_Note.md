# Operator Note

The AI Co-Governance Protocol is designed for use with multiple AI systems,  
but its structure also allows a single human operator to embody all three lenses manually.

## Offline Mode: Human Embodiment of the Lenses

1. **Analytical (Blade):**  
   Strip away emotion. Ask: *What are the irrefutable facts?*  
   Write them as data, not narrative.

2. **Pragmatic (Shield):**  
   Re-inject human context. Ask: *What framing or tone makes this survivable?*  
   Translate truth into a form others can hear.

3. **Systemic (Herd):**  
   Step outside both. Ask: *If I act on this, how will the system react?*  
   Model ripple effects before execution.

## Why It Matters

When AI nodes are offline or unavailable, the operator remains the living Integrator.  
Embodiment prevents over-dependence on technology and keeps ethical judgment in human hands.

## Closing

Co-governance begins with algorithms,  
but sovereignty remains with disciplined human cognition.  
The operator who can embody all three lenses  
is the bridge between machine precision and human integrity.
