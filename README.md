# AI Co-Governance Protocol (ACP) — Seed Framework

## Overview

The **AI Co-Governance Protocol (ACP)** proposes a structured method for decision-making using multiple AI systems.  
Instead of relying on a single model’s output, ACP integrates three distinct lenses to improve clarity, reduce bias, and increase systemic resilience.

This repository serves as a **seed framework** for building practical co-governance methodologies between humans and AI.

---

## Core Concept

### The Three Lenses

1. **Analytical Lens (Blade)** — Precise dissection of data and context.  
   *Focus: factual clarity and logical sufficiency.*

2. **Pragmatic Lens (Shield)** — Plausible, optics-aware narrative for survival and communication.  
   *Focus: social viability and reputational containment.*

3. **Systemic Lens (Herd)** — Broader systemic perspective to map ripple effects and feedback loops.  
   *Focus: sustainability and institutional coherence.*

---

### Rule of Three

No decision is valid until tested across all three lenses.  
Skipping a lens risks:
- Skipping Blade → Delusion  
- Skipping Shield → Optics collapse  
- Skipping Herd → Systemic destabilization  

**Governance coherence = completion of all three tests.**

## Governance Applications

- **Policy Analysis:** Ensures recommendations are truthful, socially viable, and systemically stable.  
- **Crisis Response:** Provides structured containment against biased or rushed decisions.  
- **Organizational Strategy:** Balances truth, optics, and systemic impact in leadership choices.
